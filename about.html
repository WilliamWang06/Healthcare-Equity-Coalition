Who We Are

What is the Healthcare Equity Coalition?

The Healthcare Equity Coalition (HEC) is a student-led organization pioneered at Brown University that advances healthcare equity in the United States by partnering with local community health organizations, advocating for meaningful health equity policy changes, and co-developing multilingual health education materials to close gaps in access and outcomes.

Mission

To close the gaps in healthcare access facing underrepresented and underserved communities.

Vision

To create a world where every person has equal access to quality healthcare.

Values

Equity in healthcare, solidarity within our community, and empowerment through education.
