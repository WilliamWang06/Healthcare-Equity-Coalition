What's Next

Potential projects & collaborations

Early-stage initiatives we're planning to pursue with our partners, organized by pillar.

Public Education

Reaching communities with clear information

  • Working with RI Dept. of Health to distribute translated health flyers
  • Plain-language explainer on rising insurance costs, ahead of a state hearing
  • On-campus info sessions about local health resources and how to access them
Community Support

Hands-on service with our partners

  • Volunteering with local Health Equity Zones (HEZ) that need extra hands
  • Trained interpreters helping at Clínica Esperanza and health events
  • Youth health workshops at Providence community centers — nutrition, mental health, first aid
Political Advocacy

A student voice in the statehouse

  • Student testimony at hearings for bills limiting corporate control of healthcare (S2459, S2492, S2950)
  • Plain-language bill explainers and op-eds for other students
  • Sign-on letter showing student support, delivered to legislators
