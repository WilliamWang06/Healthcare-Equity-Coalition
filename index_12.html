Who We Are

What is the Health Equity Coalition?

The Health Equity Coalition (HEC) is a student-led organization pioneered at Brown University that advances healthcare equity in the United States by partnering with local community health organizations, advocating for meaningful health equity policy changes, and co-developing multilingual health education materials to close gaps in access and outcomes.

Mission

To close the gaps in healthcare access facing underrepresented and underserved communities.

Vision

To create a world where every person has equal access to quality healthcare.

Values

Equity in healthcare, solidarity within our community, and empowerment through education.

Our Approach

Three pillars, one mission

Everything HEC does falls under public education, community support, or political advocacy — working together to close the gap in healthcare access.

Public Education

  • Create accessible content that breaks down complex healthcare policy
  • Host workshops, panels, and speaker events on healthcare access and rights
  • Develop shareable, multilingual resources that partner organizations can use

Community Support

  • Translate partnerships into hands-on service — clinic support & insurance navigation
  • Assist with translation, interpretation & resource coordination for non-English speakers
  • Mobilize the community through campus & cross-campus collaborative events

Political Advocacy

  • Work directly with legislators & state agencies to advance statewide healthcare equity legislation
  • Research policy issues and prepare materials for stakeholder meetings
  • Track legislative sessions to find moments where student voices matter most

Bills we're following

Three 2026 Rhode Island Senate bills aimed at reining in corporate consolidation of healthcare — the focus of our political advocacy pillar this session. Our full interactive tracker launches with the site.

Full Tracker Coming Soon
S2459 Held for Study
RI Ban on the Corporate Practice of Medicine Act
Would prohibit corporations from directly practicing medicine or controlling physicians' clinical decisions in Rhode Island.
Introduced 2/6/26 · Lead sponsor Sen. Linda Ujifusa + 8 co-sponsors · Senate Health & Human Services held for further study 3/3/26
S2492 Held for Study
Healthcare Material Change Notice Act
Requires healthcare entities to give the RI Attorney General & Dept. of Health 180 days' written notice before major transactions like mergers or acquisitions.
Introduced 2/6/26 · Lead sponsor Sen. Linda Ujifusa + 9 co-sponsors · Senate Judiciary held for further study 5/7/26
S2950 Held for Study
Private Equity & REIT Healthcare Investment Oversight
Requires private equity firms & REITs investing in RI healthcare providers to notify the AG of material changes and post a bond with the Dept. of Health.
Introduced 3/4/26 · Lead sponsor Sen. Lori Urso + 7 co-sponsors · Senate Judiciary held for further study 5/7/26

"Held for further study" is the RI Senate's common procedural step for pausing a bill without a formal vote — sponsors can reintroduce it next session. Source: Rhode Island General Assembly bill records.
